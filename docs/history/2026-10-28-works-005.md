# works-005: five games, gathered (2026-10-28)

The owner regroups the old worlds and games into numbered repositories `works-NNN-…`; this is the fifth: five worlds on
engine 3 and their bespoke games — Scarlet Sails, Around the World in 80 Days, Cinema Paradiso, The Lady with the Dog,
The Elder Son.

## What and how

- **Gathered with their histories**, each from its `env/dev` (`git filter-repo --to-subdirectory-filter`, then
  `git merge --allow-unrelated-histories` into `main`, one merge commit each): `moood-me/pavel--<slug>` →
  `worlds/<slug>/`, `pavel--game--<slug>` → `games/<slug>/`, for `scarlet-sails`, `around-the-world-in-80-days`,
  `cinema-paradiso`, `lady-with-the-dog`, `elder-son`.
- **Not taken:** the earlier versions (`alye-parusa`, `alye-parusa-v2`, `game--alye-parusa`, `vokrug-sveta`,
  `vokrug-sveta-v2`).
- **As moood has them now** (moood's `check` passes): worlds and games declare `"languages": ["en", "ru"]`; a game's
  `"world"` is the world's path (`../../worlds/<slug>`); `people.json` without `name` (a person is `{voice, how,
  short}`); Saville Row's own film says its two lines with `{voice, how}` — who says them folded into `how`; The Lady
  with the Dog's room and bazaar music at 60 bpm (Lyria's least; they asked for 56); the games' own
  `bespoke/check/run.mjs` names its report's printer `print`, not `say` (the language's own name); the old moood's
  `AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md` removed; translations laid out by `check --write`.
- **Docs**, the earlier versions' included: `docs/alye-parusa/`, `docs/vokrug-sveta/`, `docs/drama-games/` (all but
  `ostatok-dnya`, a game never built — it stays in `pavel-zhur/rpg`) from `pavel-zhur/rpg`; their history from
  `moood-me/studio` (`docs/history/`). References to docs elsewhere are written `<owner>/<repo>:<path>`.
- The old repositories are left as they are.
