# 2026-10-29 — Polish (`pl`)

Why: Павел: «и польский» — Polish as a further language beside the nine.

- The five games and their worlds: `"pl"` in `languages`, every world's and scene's `lang/pl.json` (and
  `worlds/scarlet-sails/modules/closeups.pl.json`), each game's `bespoke/lang/pl/strings.json` (1309 texts).
- Titles and names as the Polish editions have them: «W 80 dni dookoła świata» (Fileas Fogg, Obieżyświat, Fix, Auda,
  Klub Reforma), «Cinema Paradiso» (Totò, Alfredo, Elena), «Starszy syn» (Busygin, Sarafanow, Silwa), «Dama z
  pieskiem» (Gurow, Anna Siergiejewna, Oreanda, Słowiański Bazar), «Szkarłatne żagle» (Assol, Artur Grey, Longren,
  Egl, Kaperna; the village's taunt «czerwone żagle» kept apart from the book's «szkarłatne»).
- Register: the player is «ty». Characters address each other as the books do: Gurow and Anna «pan/pani» in Yalta,
  «ty» from Oreanda on; Busygin «ty» to Sarafanow while he plays the son, «pan» when he confesses; Fix's ingratiating
  «pan» to Obieżyświat. The replay and map words are those of the other Polish games.
- Seen, not changed: the games' embedded title and hand fonts (`games/*/bespoke/fonts.css`) cover Latin-1 and
  Cyrillic only — ą ć ę ł ń ś ź ż fall back to another font inside a word.

How it was checked: moood's `check` (its `pl` branch: `pl` in `i18n/languages.json`), `node packages/check/cli.js <repo> --write`
laid the files out, the translations were written into them, then `check` on the whole repository says «all right».
From the English source, the Russian read beside it as a second view of meaning and tone. Only translations and the
`languages` lists were added; no source text, code or doc was changed.
