# Korean (`ko`) (2026-10-29)

Why: «Павел: и корейский». Korean is one more language beside the nine there. moood knows `ko` (its branch `ko`).

## What

The five worlds and their five games (Around the World in 80 Days, Cinema Paradiso, The Elder Son, The Lady with the
Dog, Scarlet Sails) now have `"ko"` at the end of `"languages"`. Every `ko` file was laid out by `check --write` and
filled: the worlds' `lang/ko.json`, their scenes' `scenes/<folder>/lang/ko.json`, Scarlet Sails'
`modules/closeups.ko.json`, and the games' `bespoke/lang/ko/strings.json`. The cask's three lines are in Korean
word order: 그레이가 / 낙원에 있을 때 / 나를 마시리라. No game has other data by language. Titles that say "v3" keep it.

Translated from the English, with the Russian as a second view. Narration is in literary plain style (해라체).
Choices and labels are short nouns or `-기` forms, and instructions to the player are in 해요체. Each character speaks
at their own level, kept the same towards each listener: Fogg uses 하게체 to Passepartout and 합쇼체 to Aouda and
strangers. Gurov goes from 합쇼체 to 하오체 with Anna, and she uses 해요체 throughout. Children and family speak
반말. Plurals have the one Korean form (`other`).

Names. Titles and main names are those fixed for all repositories, the rest are from Korean editions where known,
otherwise transcribed by 외래어 표기법:

- 『80일간의 세계 일주』: 필리어스 포그, 파스파르투, 픽스, 아우다, 리폼 클럽, 새빌 로, 펠멜 거리, 키우니 (the elephant),
  크로머티, 스튜어트, 번스비, 바툴카르, 머지, 스피디; ships 몽골리아호, 랑군호, 카르나틱호, 탕카데르호,
  제너럴 그랜트호, 차이나호, 헨리에타호. The Yokohama troupe is 긴코 곡예단 (긴코들).
- 『시네마 천국』: 토토, 알프레도, 엘레나, 살바토레, 마리아, 아델피오 신부, 치초, 안나, 잔칼도; the cinema 시네마
  파라디소 (파라디소). The slate «ALFREDO» stays in Latin letters, because the world's code counts its letters.
- 『장남』 (밤필로프): 부시긴, 실바, 사라파노프 (안드레이 그리고리예비치), 니나, 바센카 (바샤), 볼로댜, 쿠디모프
  (미샤), 마카르스카야 (나탈리야), 갈랴 부시기나.
- 「개를 데리고 다니는 여인」: 구로프 (드미트리 드미트리치), 안나 세르게예브나, 폰 디데리츠, 얄타, 오레안다, S시,
  스피츠, 슬라뱐스키 바자르.
- 『붉은 돛』: 아솔, 아서 그레이, 롱그렌, 에글, 카페르나, 리스, 비밀호, 메너스, 릴리언, 고프, 레티카, 판텐, 힌, 필립.
  The children's and Hin's teasing "Red sails" is 빨간 돛, and the true sails are 붉은/진홍빛 돛.
