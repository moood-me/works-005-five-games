# works-005: Spanish (2026-10-29)

Why: «Павел: ещё язык — испанский». A third language beside English (the source) and Russian, on branch `es`.

## What

- All five worlds and their games — Around the World in 80 Days, Cinema Paradiso, The Elder Son, The Lady with the Dog,
  Scarlet Sails — declare `"languages": ["en", "ru", "es"]`; the es files laid out by `check --write` and filled: the
  worlds' and scenes' `lang/es.json`, Scarlet Sails' `modules/closeups.es.json`, each game's
  `bespoke/lang/es/strings.json`. The games have no other per-language data.
- Translated from the English, with the Russian as a second view; neutral Spanish, «tú» for the player. Names as
  Spanish editions have them: «La vuelta al mundo en ochenta días» (Phileas Fogg, Picaporte, Aouda, Fix, the
  «Tankadera», las Narices Largas), «Cinema Paradiso» (Totò, Alfredo, Elena, Salvatore; Alfredo's lines as the film
  has them), «El hijo mayor» (Vampílov: Busiguin, Sarafánov, Vásenka, Silva, Nina, Kudímov), «La dama del perrito»
  (Chéjov: Gúrov, Anna Serguéyevna, Von Dideritz), «Velas escarlata» (Grin: Assol, Grey, Longren, Egle, Kaperna,
  «El Secreto»). Nothing in the code, the sources or the Russian changed.

## How checked

moood's `check` (branch `es` of moood) on the whole repository: «all right — 1309 texts in 67 scopes». The games' own
checks (`bespoke/check/run.mjs`) play the English only and take no language, so they were not run for es.
