# Traditional Chinese (`zh-hant`) (2026-10-29)

Why: «Павел: ещё язык — традиционный китайский».

## What

The five worlds and their five games — Around the World in 80 Days, Cinema Paradiso, The Elder Son, The Lady with
the Dog, Scarlet Sails — declare `"languages": ["en", "ru", "zh-hant"]`; every text has its Traditional Chinese, as
written in Taiwan, from the English source with the Russian beside it: the scenes' `lang/zh-hant.json`, Scarlet Sails'
`modules/closeups.zh-hant.json` (the cask's three lines), the worlds' `lang/zh-hant.json`, the games'
`bespoke/lang/zh-hant/strings.json`. No game here has other data by language. Titles that say "v3" keep it, as the
Russian does.

Names, as Taiwan's editions and releases have them where they were found:

- 《環遊世界八十天》: 菲利斯·福格, 萬事通, 費克斯, 艾娥達; 改革俱樂部, 薩佛街, 基烏尼 (the elephant).
- 《新天堂樂園》 (the Taiwan release's title): 多多 (Totò), 沙瓦托 (Salvatore), 艾佛特 (Alfredo), 愛蓮娜 (Elena);
  the cinema 天堂戲院, the new one 新天堂戲院; 姜卡多 (Giancaldo).
- 《長子》 (萬比洛夫): 布西金, 薩拉法諾夫, 西爾瓦, 妮娜, 瓦先卡, 庫季莫夫, 馬卡爾斯卡婭.
- 《帶小狗的女士》 (契訶夫, the Taiwan title of 丘光's translation): 古羅夫 (德米特里·德米特里奇), 安娜·謝爾蓋耶芙娜,

- 《紅帆》 (亞歷山大·格林): 阿索莉, 格雷 (亞瑟), 隆格倫, 埃格爾, 卡佩爾納, 「祕密號」.
