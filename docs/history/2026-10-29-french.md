Why: Павел: ещё язык — французский.

- The five worlds and five games are in French too: `"fr"` in their `languages`; `lang/fr.json`, each scene's
  `scenes/<folder>/lang/fr.json`, modules' `modules/<name>.fr.json`, the games' `bespoke/lang/fr/strings.json`.
  Translated from the English, the Russian read beside it (for Chekhov, Vampilov and Grin — as the original's tone);
  «tu» for the player in the interface.

  (le Reform-Club, Saville-row, Passepartout, Fix, Mrs. Aouda; chapter subtitles «dans lequel…»).
- «Cinema Paradiso»: Totò, Alfredo, Elena; Italian signs stay Italian (ALFREDO), the envelope's address «Palermo».
- «La Dame au petit chien»: Gourov, Anna Serguéïevna, Oréanda; «vous» in Yalta, «tu» later, as in Chekhov.
- «Le Fils aîné»: Bousyguine, Sarafanov, Silva, Vassenka, Nina, Koudimov.
- «Les Voiles écarlates»: Assol, Gray, Longren, Kaperna, Egl, le « Secret »; the teasing «voiles rouges» kept apart
  from «écarlates»; the cask's inscription (drawn) translated, as in Russian.
