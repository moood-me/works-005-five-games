# The beds’ recordings

Real recordings from Freesound, licence CC0 (no conditions): from each a piece of ~45 s is cut and looped (its last
2.5 s mixed into its beginning), its loudness brought to −24 dBFS RMS, MP3 96 kbit/s. Chosen by Pavel on 2026-10-16
(rpg docs/history/2026-10-16-game-sounds.md); `sound.js` fits each to the loudness of the bed it replaced.
